Government Screening Information System

Developing and supporting screening applications for federal- and state-level government operations to standardise information collection and categorisation workflows.

Problem

Screening operations need a consistent, structured way to capture and categorise information across different locations and levels of government.

Professional role

Developed and supported screening applications and their database components, and supported users in operational settings.

Approach

Requirements were translated into structured digital forms and workflow steps, backed by a relational database with controlled access, so that information is captured the same way wherever screening takes place.

Operational relevance

Structured digital processing reduces variation in how information is recorded and makes categorisation workflows easier to follow and review.

Key technical components

  • Requirements and workflow design
  • Web application (C#, ASP.NET MVC)
  • Relational database and T-SQL logic
  • Access control
  • User training and support
