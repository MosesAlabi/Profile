Field Information Systems

Field Data Collection and Geospatial Insight

Supporting digital surveys, field data quality assurance, community mapping, and geospatial outputs for operational assessments and monitoring.

Problem

Field assessments depend on well-structured collection tools and geographic data that is accurate enough to act on.

Professional role

Designed digital survey tools, trained field staff, coordinated surveys, and performed GIS data quality assurance.

Approach

Structured KoboToolbox forms capture consistent field data; quality-assurance checks catch errors early; ArcGIS and QGIS turn validated data into maps for assessments and monitoring.

Operational relevance

Linking structured collection, quality assurance, and geospatial analysis gives operational teams a clearer picture on the ground.

Key technical components

  • KoboToolbox survey design
  • Field staff training
  • Geographic data QA
  • Community mapping
  • Monitoring outputs
