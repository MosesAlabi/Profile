About Moses Alabi

Data, databases and information systems — built to be trusted.

My focus is not simply collecting data. It is making information consistent, secure, and useful to the people who depend on it.

Moses Alabi is a data, database, and information systems professional with more than 11 years of experience across information management, application development, digital data workflows, analytics, and programme operations with the International Organization for Migration (IOM) in Nigeria.

His professional experience includes relational database development, SQL Server administration, data integration, data quality assurance, government-facing screening applications, dashboards, geospatial reporting, digital data-collection workflows, and operational decision support.

He holds an MSc in Computing and Information Systems with Distinction from Liverpool John Moores University, United Kingdom.

His academic research examined cybersecurity in cloud computing, including security threats, mitigation strategies, governance frameworks, and emerging approaches. This experience supports his interest in secure, reliable, well-governed, and human-centred information systems.

His broader interests include data engineering, trustworthy digital infrastructure, responsible AI, decision-support systems, and technology that helps public-sector and humanitarian organisations make better decisions.
